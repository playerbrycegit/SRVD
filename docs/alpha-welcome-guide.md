# Welcome to the STATION Private Alpha

## What STATION is

STATION is a professional tool built for the person behind the bar — not guests, not managers.
Right now it covers three things: logging your shifts and tips, a private space to save your
recipes, and a few calculators built for real bar math (batch scaling, ABV/proof, unit conversion).

## What's currently included

- Account creation with email verification
- Logging shifts (date, cash tips, card tips) and seeing your lifetime/average/best stats
- A weekly tip goal with progress tracking
- A private recipe vault — name, category, ingredients, method, notes, searchable
- Three calculators: batch scaling, ABV/proof, unit conversion
- Password reset if you forget your password

## What's not included yet

Being direct about this rather than vague: there's no way to *edit* a shift or recipe once saved
(you'd delete and re-add it), no Settings screen yet (time zone, currency, and units aren't
configurable), no checklists, no shift journal, no ingredient library, no recipe costing, no
collections, and no education/learning content. These are real ideas we're tracking, not secrets —
they're just not built, and we're not going to tell you they're "coming soon" because we don't have
a date and don't want to promise one.

## Test-period expectations

We're asking you to use STATION the way you'd actually use it — logging real shifts, saving real
recipes, running real calculations — for the length of the test period we agree on together. There's
no minimum daily requirement; use it when it's actually useful to you, and tell us when it isn't.

## How to report a bug

Use the feedback option in the app (Settings/Help area once you're in). Tell us what you expected
to happen, what actually happened, and roughly how to reproduce it if you can. If something feels
like a security or privacy problem specifically, say so explicitly and we'll treat it with priority.

## How to submit feedback

Same feedback form covers bugs, confusing moments, feature ideas, and things you genuinely like —
all of it is useful. Be honest, including if something feels unnecessary or annoying.

## Privacy guidance

Please don't paste passwords, session tokens, or anything you wouldn't want a developer to read
into a feedback report — we do have an automatic filter that redacts anything that looks like a
token, but the safest move is just not including it in the first place. Your shift data and recipes
are private to your account; we're not looking at your data casually, only investigating a specific
bug report if you've told us about one that touches that data.
